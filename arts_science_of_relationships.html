Module 1: Relationship in Our Lives

Module 1.1: Why do people get involved in relationships

People get involved in relationships to address their needs.
Need: Required for life
  • Physical/Biological: Oxygen, water, food, stimulation, activity, rest, physical safety.
  • Psychological: Security, pleasure, comfort, identity, self-esteem, achievement.
  • Social: Affiliation, intimacy, acceptance, community
Want: Preferences for life
  • Ranges of representations of Needs

Module 1.6: Individualized N3C

Module 1.7 N3C: Circumstances

3 levels of Circumstances:
  1. Macro Level: global.
    major global events, historic demography associated with one's generation.
  2. Mezzo Level: regional/local.
    sociocultral reality (social circle, reference groups, cultural values).
  3. Micro Level: personal.
    employment status, health condition, life stage, relationship status.

Module 1.8 N3C: Characteristics

3 types of characteristics:
  1. Physical: height, body type, ethinicity, gender
  2. Psychological personality, values, worldview, preferences, emotional and behavioural patterns
  3. Socialcultural: lifestyle, cultural practice, religious practice

Module 1.10 Differences & Biodiversity

Relationships always involve differences.
Being able to deal with differences is key to succeed in any relationship.
Managing diversity is important individual growth.

Module 1.11 Similarities & Differences

There's no categorical answer to find people similar or different. What works changes from one relationship to another. The balance can also change over time in the same relationship.
The key is the ability to deal with differences in relationships.

Module 1.12 Capacity to deal with differences

Capacity: ability to deal with differences in a constructed manner.
Differences will continue to emerge as you know more about the other person in relationships.
In an ideal relationship, people should be changing and growing.
Develop interest and curiosity towards differences.

Module 2: Getting Started

Module 2.1 Social Scripts

People tend to follow some social scripts, whether from one culture or another. We should be aware of the script we follow and be open about the opportunity of authoring our own script.
Social Scripts:
  1. The set of behaviors that people follow.
  2. May not meet your needs.
  3. People fall into these traps.

Module 2.2 Social Scripts and Traps

In different periods, people have different expectations toward what relationship looks like.
Each society has its own love scripts. Each of them carry its own traps.

Module 2.3 Social Scripts and Traps: Consumerism

Some people have disappointment toward their relationships because they do not think of what they need, but rather follow what everyone else do.
  1. Consumerism: buying instead of sensing and meeting the sensitivity of the partner's specific needs and circumstances.
